## A note on the JLPT levels

The JLPT has not published official kanji lists since it was reorganised
into five levels in 2010. The N5..N1 assignment used here is Jonathan
Waller's widely used reconstruction, as redistributed by kanji-data. It is
an informed approximation and no more: expect a kanji here and there to sit
one level away from where a given textbook puts it.
